Research Philosophy

The Alakaapuri Stock Selection Framework

We avoid speculative tips, operators, and penny stocks. Every recommendation passes through 4 strict institutional filters.

01
Economic Moat & Pricing Power

Companies with durable competitive advantages, brand monopolies, or high switching costs that protect profit margins from inflation.

02
Clean Promoter Governance

Pristine corporate integrity, transparent capital allocation, zero auditor red flags, and low or zero promoter share pledges.

03
High Capital Efficiency (ROCE > 18%)

Consistent Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) and Free Cash Flow generation that fuels debt-free organic expansion.

04
Margin of Safety & Valuation

Buying industry leaders at reasonable multiples relative to their growth runway — safeguarding capital against market drawdowns.
