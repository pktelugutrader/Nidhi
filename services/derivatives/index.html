Core Methodologies

Capital Preservation Comes First

Derivatives are powerful instruments when used for hedging and defined-risk positioning. We strip away reckless gambling and replace it with mathematical discipline.

Portfolio Downside Hedging

Shield your long-term equity or mutual fund portfolio from major market crashes (Budget, elections, global shocks) using index put options and beta-hedging.

  • Insure equity value without selling
  • Avoid capital gains tax triggered by exits
  • Cost-effective insurance against black swans
Defined-Risk Option Spreads

Multi-leg strategies (Bull Call Spreads, Bear Put Spreads, Iron Condors) with strictly pre-defined maximum risk and high probability of profit.

  • Capped downside on every trade
  • Exploits implied volatility skew
  • Rule-based entry and exit parameters
Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)

Proprietary order-flow analysis tracking institutional buying and selling volume to identify true market direction before breakouts occur.

  • Institutional absorption detection
  • Spot divergences between price and volume
  • Available on TradingView
Options Greeks & Volatility Analysis

Deep understanding of Delta, Theta, Gamma and Vega to structure positions that benefit from time decay and volatility contraction.

  • India VIX cycle evaluation
  • Non-directional income strategies
  • Continuous risk monitoring
